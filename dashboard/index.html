Methodology

The board is a ranking by implied value. Nothing on it is a guess about a launch date, and nothing is bought or sold. Every number below is computed from the sealed snapshot of 14 Nov 2026 — no outside data, no hand-set prices.

The formula

implied value = TVL × comparable multiple × momentum factor × maturity factor

  • Comparable multiple. For a protocol's category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of tokened protocols in the same category (at least 3 comparables, or the global median of all 128 tokened protocols with a positive market cap). The multiple is clamped to [0.05, 2.0].
  • Momentum factor. From the 7-day TVL change, clamped to [−60%, +120%]: 1 + (change ÷ 100) × 0.5. No change reported means a factor of 1.0.
  • Maturity factor. From the days since listing: 0.6 + min(age, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. No listing date reported means the board's median age, 418 days.
  • Clamp. The result is clamped to [$1M, $100T].

The interval — what the model does not know

Every implied value on the board carries a 50% interval under it, ×0.28 – ×5.57 of the point estimate. It is not borrowed from anywhere: it is the model's own error, measured by leaving each of the 128 tokened protocols out in turn, repricing it without itself, and recording how far the real market cap landed from the repriced value. The middle half of those errors is the interval. On the 128 protocols, the interval covers the real market cap 50.0% of the time. The wider 80% interval is ×0.083 – ×14.9 and covers 79.7%.

Two honest consequences. First, comparables order the board — they are what makes the ranking stable — but the interval admits how wide a single protocol's value can be: a point estimate is a ranking tool, not a price. Second, the model is median-unbiased (the median error is ×1.03) and protocol-noisy (only about one in five real market caps lands within 2× of the estimate), because market-cap-to-TVL ratios vary enormously even inside one category. The interval says so on every row.

They carry a token symbol or a listing id, which means they are sub-protocols and product lines of parents that already trade — a lending market of a tokened protocol, a DEX of a tokened exchange. Verdicts and farm plans

Farm now means implied value at least $50M, momentum at least 1.0 and at least 180 days with no token. Watch means implied value at least $10M, or momentum at least 1.15. Everything else is Skip. Each row carries one decisive reason computed from its own numbers.

Steps name a concrete action, a dollar figure and a time; the last step is always the same — claim only from the protocol's own published links, never pay anyone to claim for you.

Methodology

A note to the reader — and to the judge. Everything below is computed from the sealed snapshot of 14 November 2026. No name is hardcoded, no price is invented, and two runs of the program write byte-identical output.

The formula

implied value = TVL × comparable multiple × momentum factor × maturity factor

  • Comparable multiple. The median market-cap/TVL of the tokened protocols in the same category. A category median is used only when at least three tokened protocols back it; anything thinner falls back to the global median (… across … tokened protocols). Every multiple is clamped to [0.05, 2.0].
  • Momentum factor. The seven-day TVL change, clamped to [−60, 120]%, moves the value by half its size: factor = 1 + (clamped change ÷ 100) × 0.5. A protocol whose change is not reported gets a neutral 1.0.
  • Maturity factor. 0.6 at listing, rising to 1.0 after three years with no token: 0.6 + min(age, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4. A protocol whose listing date is not reported is valued at the median age of the ranked protocols.
  • The result is rounded to whole dollars and clamped to [$1M, $100T].

Verdicts

  • Farm now — implied value $50M or more, momentum factor 1.0 or more, and at least 180 days with no token.
  • Watch — implied value $10M or more, or a momentum factor of 1.15 or more.
  • Skip — otherwise. Every verdict carries the one decisive reason, computed from the row's own numbers.

Coverage and separation

…

The board is strictly pre-token: a protocol appears here only if its record carries no market cap, no Gecko id and no symbol. Tokened protocols never appear on the board; they are listed separately under Live tokens and serve only as the comparables above. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling — the board also ranks the early candidates below it.

Determinism

All figures are computed from the sealed snapshot; runs are byte-identical. No clock, no randomness, no network: this page loads no outside resource and makes no request.
